# GetYourGuide listing draft (English)

## Title
Tashkent: Private Mountain Day – Amirsoy, Chimgan, Ancient Plane Trees & Charvak Lake

## Short description (≈ 200 characters)
Escape to the Tian Shan mountains with a private driver-guide. Ride the Amirsoy gondola, taste fresh kok somsa, see 850-year-old plane trees and relax by turquoise Charvak Lake.

## Highlights
- Ride the Amirsoy gondola to 2,290 m for sweeping views of the Western Tian Shan
- Arrive early and skip the crowds: private hotel pickup at 07:30
- Taste kok somsa, a green-herb pastry baked in a clay tandoor, with local tea
- Walk among 850-year-old plane trees and ancient petroglyphs in Khojikent village
- Unwind with tea on a traditional tapchan by turquoise Charvak Lake

## Full description
Leave the city behind for a full day in the mountains of Tashkent region, just for you and your companions (up to 3 people).

Our first stop is Gazalkent for freshly baked kok somsa and tea, a local breakfast most visitors never try. After a quick photo stop at the Charvak dam, we reach Amirsoy Mountain Resort early, before the tour buses. Take the modern gondola up to 2,290 m and enjoy the views from the upper station.

After lunch in a mountain restaurant (local trout, plov or shashlik), we visit the Chimgan viewpoint facing Big Chimgan peak. Horse riding is available here if you want it. In the afternoon we drive to Khojikent village to see its giant plane trees, some around 850 years old, and rock carvings thousands of years old. You can also ride the Chinorkent cable car to a 1,680 m viewpoint.

We end the day on the shore of Charvak Lake with tea on a tapchan before driving back to your hotel.

Your driver-guide knows the best photo spots and is happy to take pictures of you along the way.

## Important information
- What to bring: comfortable shoes, a warm layer (it is 8–10 °C cooler at the top of Amirsoy), sunglasses, sunscreen, cash in UZS, passport
- The Amirsoy gondola may close in strong wind; if it does, we replace it with a Gulkam Canyon walk or the Chinorkent cable car
- From December to March the route runs in reverse order, and snow activities replace the lake stop
- Not suitable for wheelchair users
